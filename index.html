Open source · A-share research2026

QR Factor Lab

A small, inspectable framework for turning brokerage reports and public factor formulas into timed, reproducible experiments.

The research log indexes 19 brokerage reports and 34 factor ideas. Eleven daily-bar formulas were screened on two small historical samples. The results are exploratory: one factor changed direction across samples, and the report-based volume strategy did not beat its sample baseline after assumed costs.

Signals use information available after close; the test label starts at the next open. Static universes and retrospectively adjusted data prevent a live performance claim. Raw licensed market data is excluded from the public repository.

Research project2025–2026

Macroeconomic tail-risk forecasting

Built a Growth-at-Risk evaluation framework with more than 300 U.S. and China indicators. Compared time-series foundation models with econometric and machine-learning baselines, including AR, factor models, MIDAS, LSTM, and XGBoost.

Focus: data alignment, quantile forecasts, and out-of-sample comparison. Research summary; code not public.

Mixture distributions & tail risk

Studied 3,500 simulated returns across three volatility environments, comparing a single Gaussian with a three-component mixture. Estimated 5% VaR and Expected Shortfall using 200,000 Monte Carlo draws.

Focus: distributional fit, stress behavior, and model limitations. Research summary; code not public.
