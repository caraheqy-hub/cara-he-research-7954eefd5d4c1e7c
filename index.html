QR Factor Lab

A reproducible starting point for A-share factor research.

The Python framework calculates example momentum and low-volatility factors, checks cross-sectional Rank IC, and runs a simple long-only backtest with assumed costs. Its research log records 19 brokerage reports, 34 candidate ideas, and exploratory screening of 11 daily-bar formulas.

Signals are formed after the close and tested from the next open. The small, fixed stock universe and adjusted-price snapshot limit what the results can show; this is not a live trading strategy.
